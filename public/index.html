Chess Cinema

A cinematic chess visualization tool, built by the founder, that turns chess positions and moves into cinematic visual sequences and exportable clips.

  • Interactive chessboard
  • PGN import, with a move timeline and Prev / Next / Live navigation
  • Play Animation to watch moves unfold
  • Arrows, highlights, move classification and notes
  • Story Targets that guide the camera framing
  • Cinematic capture effects
  • Single-move WebM export: 1080×1080, 30 fps, VP9

Exports one move at a time. It does not render a whole game as a single finished video. Export uses your browser's video recorder, so a recent Chromium-based browser such as Chrome is recommended.

Video Effects Lab

Deterministic, frame-accurate visual effects for short clips.

  • More than 25 effects, including Screen Shake, Punch Zoom, Speed Ramp, Freeze, Glitch Burst, Lens Flare and Whip Pan
  • Effect windows are anchored to source-video time
  • Optional hook text burned into the start of the clip
  • Preview, then export as WebM
  • Runs entirely in your browser tab; your source file is never modified

Works best in desktop Chrome (needs WebCodecs). Exports are video-only for now: audio is not carried over.

How the tools work

Browser-first

Nothing to install. Chess Cinema and Video Effects Lab run in a normal browser tab.

Your files stay on your device

Neither tool uploads your games or clips, and neither contains analytics. Chess Cinema loads one third-party script, the chess.js library, from cdnjs.

About

Chess Cinema Studio is a solo, bootstrapped project from an independent builder based in India. It started as a way to make better chess videos faster.

Both tools are early and have rough edges. Feedback from chess creators and coaches is very welcome.

How to use Chess Cinema
  1. Import a game: paste a PGN into the box and press Import PGN, or press Load sample game to try a short one.
  2. Step through moves: use ‹ Prev / Next ›, or click any move in the Move Timeline.
  3. Watch a move: select a move, then press Play Animation to replay it. Captures use the Capture FX you pick.
  4. Annotate: choose Draw Arrow, Highlight or Story Target in Mode, then drag or click on the board. Color sets the annotation color.
  5. Export: select a move and press Export Animation to save that one move as a WebM video.
